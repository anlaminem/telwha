What the test gives

Not just a result, but a clearer picture of your behavior

After completing the test, you see your archetype, strengths, risk patterns, and the first steps that make sense specifically for you.

What you will see

  • Your primary archetype and, if relevant, a second strongly expressed archetype.
  • Patterns of tension, avoidance, control, chaos, or overload.
  • Behavioral risks in habits, decisions, and money routines.
  • The first concrete actions worth starting with.

You may recognize yourself here

  • You seem to know what you should do with money, but still postpone important decisions.
  • Sometimes it feels like the issue is not the numbers, but a repeating internal pattern you cannot clearly name.
  • You do not want another generic “track your budget” tip — you want to honestly see what is happening and why it keeps repeating.
  • Crisis, war, instability, and fatigue do affect financial decisions — that part is real.
  • But under the same pressure, people respond differently: some freeze, some avoid, some rush, and some hold everything alone until exhaustion.

About FinLab

What this project is

FinLab is a financial laboratory for Ukrainians and about Ukrainians. We study common problems, scenarios, and behavioral patterns to better understand how people deal with money in real life.

Our test measures not only archetypes, but also separate scales: financial clarity, emotional spending, reserve discipline, debt dependence, delayed decisions, stress, burnout, relationship tension, and other behavioral signals. Family financial patterns can also shape adult money decisions.
Each person receives an individual result. The website displays only general anonymous statistics without personal answers from individual participants.
To better identify recurring financial patterns among Ukrainians and build more accurate insights, products, materials, and support based on real behavior rather than assumptions.
