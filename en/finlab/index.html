About FinLab

What this project is

FinLab is a financial laboratory built for Ukrainians and about Ukrainians. We study typical problems, scenarios and behavioral patterns to better understand how people actually behave with money.

The test measures not only the archetype, but also individual scales: money clarity, emotional spending, reserve discipline, debt dependence, decision delay, stress, burnout, relationship tension and other behavioral signals. Research also shows that financial models and money scripts can be transmitted across generations, so family context matters for adult money decisions.[web:79][web:90]
Each person receives their personal result. The website shows only aggregated anonymous statistics without individual responses of specific participants.
To see recurring financial scenarios of Ukrainians more clearly and to build sharper insights, products, materials and support based on behavior rather than assumptions.
