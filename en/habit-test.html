Financial habits test

Avoidance of money, overcontrol, scarcity mode, impulsive spending and behavioral support.

This is not about “good” or “bad” habits, but about what your nervous system tries to do with money when it feels overload, insecurity or pressure.

18 questions 3–5 minutes Research-based
Why this test
To notice not only “financial literacy gaps”, but also behavioral scenarios behind them.

Some people avoid money tasks because they are overloaded. Others control every small detail because it gives them a sense of safety. Some go into impulsive spending when they are exhausted. The test is about these patterns and how they support or weaken you in everyday financial behavior.
