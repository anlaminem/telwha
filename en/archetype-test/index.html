Archetypes

Each archetype shows typical logic, tension and ways of reacting to the market.

Chaos

Decisions are fast, internal structure is weak, consequences show up later.

Reptile

Impulse, protection, reaction to threat and sharp rise in tension.

Stabilizer

Control, rules, risk reduction and a drive toward structure.

Trendsetter

Spots direction faster and looks for opportunity earlier than others.

Antifragile

Under pressure does not break but adapts and becomes stronger.

Mediator

Looks for balance between logic, risk and inner tension.

Emergent

Rebuilds behaviour along the way and forms a new model of action.

Marginal

Stands at the edge of the system but often notices what others ignore.
