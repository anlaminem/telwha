Trader archetype test

See what kind of trading decisions you tend to make

This is a short test for traders that helps you see your typical decision style, reactions under market pressure, and behavioural patterns that may impact your strategy.

It does not diagnose you or replace a trading system. The goal is to give you a language for self‑observation: how you respond to risk, uncertainty, impulse and control.

careful framing
How to read it

Treat the result as a behavioural profile, not a label. It highlights tendencies, not a final truth about you as a trader.

Who this test is for

  • Traders who want to understand their decisions under pressure.
  • Those who notice repeated mistakes, blow‑ups or inconsistency in execution.
  • People who want a clearer view of their risk profile and internal logic of reactions.

What the test includes

  • Questions based on typical market and behavioural situations.
  • Focus on decision style, reactions to risk, impulse, control and uncertainty.
  • A result meant for self‑observation, not for boxing you into a type.
  • No need to provide unnecessary personal data to complete it.

What this test can help with

The most useful outcome is not “a nice archetype word”, but a clearer understanding of where you flip into impulse, over‑control, defence or, on the contrary, into a more mature decision style.

A language for self‑observation

The test helps you name what in live trading often feels like “chaos” or “I don’t understand why I did this at all”.

A base for next steps

The result can be a starting point for work on strategy, discipline, risk or recurring behavioural breakdowns.

Realistic focus

The test doesn’t promise profitability and doesn’t replace a system. It highlights how you tend to move into both mistakes and strong decisions.

Cautious interpretation

Archetypes are a way to structure patterns, not to reduce you to one word. The best way to use them is together with the context of your real trading.

Archetypes

Each archetype shows a typical logic of decisions, the type of tension and style of response to the market. These are short guides, not rigid boxes.

Chaos

Decisions are fast, internal structure is weaker, and consequences often catch up after the action.

Reptile

Impulse, defence, quick reaction to threat and a sharp spike in tension under pressure.

Stabilizer

Control, rules, risk reduction and a strong need for clear structure in decisions.

Trendsetter

Spots direction earlier and looks for opportunities ahead of the crowd.

Antifragile

Doesn’t just survive pressure, but is able to adapt and use it to build resilience.

Mediator

Looks for balance between logic, risk, adaptation and internal tension.

Emergent

Rebuilds behaviour in the process and gradually forms a new decision model.

Marginal

Stands at the edge of the system, but often notices what others prefer to ignore.
