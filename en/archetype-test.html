What you get

An archetype profile, hints for your strategy, self‑reflection and more clarity about your market behaviour.

Who this test is for

  • Traders who want to understand their decisions under pressure.
  • Those who notice repeating mistakes or unstable results.
  • People who want a clearer view of their risks and strengths.

What is inside

  • Archetypes based on research in financial behaviour and neurophysiology.
  • Questions framed as real market situations.
  • No personal data — only a unique ID.
  • The result shows how you actually make trading decisions.
