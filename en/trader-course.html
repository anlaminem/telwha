What emotional trading looks like in practice

In trading, decisions are taken in the moment — under pressure, after a streak of losses, in a tired state.

  • FOMO pushes you to enter late — you buy near the highs because “what if it goes further”.
  • After 2–3 stops you start “taking revenge” on the market, increase size and break your risk management.
  • Profit earned in the morning disappears in the afternoon when the brain is already on autopilot.
  • You hold losing positions “because it hurts to fix them”, and close profitable ones too early.
