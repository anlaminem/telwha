Cases

What I do

  • Analyze your financial behavior: how you think, feel and act under money pressure.
  • Show repeating decision patterns in stress, time pressure and information noise.
  • Combine work with the nervous system (“fight/flight/freeze”) with concrete money actions.
  • Map this onto financial tools: budgets, portfolios, strategies, risk management.

How I am different

  • I work not only with emotions, but also with thinking, habits and nervous system scenarios.
  • I see not just a “failed trade” or “impulse buy”, but the mechanism that leads to it.
  • I connect psychological work with numbers: trading results, spending, returns of instruments.
  • I focus not on boosting motivation, but on stable rules and structure for money decisions.
