Cases

What I do

  • I analyze financial behavior: how you think, feel and act under money pressure.
  • I identify recurring decision patterns under stress, time pressure and information noise.
  • I combine work with the nervous system (“fight / flight / freeze”) with concrete money actions.
  • I map this onto financial tools: budgets, portfolios, strategies and risk management.

How I differ

  • I work not only with emotions, but also with thinking patterns, habits and nervous system responses.
  • I see not just a “failed trade” or an “impulse purchase”, but the mechanism behind it.
  • I connect psychological work with numbers: trading results, spending and investment returns.
  • I focus not on boosting motivation, but on stable rules and structure for money decisions.
