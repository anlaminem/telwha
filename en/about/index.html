Who I am

I am a financial psychologist and behavioral analyst. I combine work with the nervous system and cognitive biases with experience in trading, investing and data analysis.

What matters to me is not only how you distribute money, but also the context in which it happens: war, labor market, previous attempts, body state, relationships and the environment you live in.

How I can help

  • Identify where your thinking and emotional patterns “break” your strategy and keep leading to the same financial outcomes.
  • Turn your numbers, journals and decision history into a clear map of risk, mistakes and growth points.
  • Build a system of actions and tools that your nervous system can sustain long term, not only through willpower.

What TELWHA is

TELWHA is six layers through which I look at life, decisions and processes: from the nervous system and biases to tools, data, money and the context of your day.

T — Technological Integration

Technology and tools

We bring in financial tools, services, AI and dashboards. Decisions should live in systems and processes, not only in your head.

E — Emotional Intelligence

Emotions and the nervous system

We look at how stress, fatigue, traumatic experience and expectations affect risk, money and daily decisions, and build protection against breakdowns.

L — Layered Architecture

System layers

Capital, cash flow, habits, relationships and work processes are broken into layers so there is no single bucket called “everything about money and life”.

W — Way & Strategy

Path and strategy

Strategy is created to find the shortest and still realistic path to change, not to make life harder with even stricter rules.

H — Holistic Approach

Wholeness

We do not separate money and decisions from the body, relationships, work and environment. No “correct” financial decision should damage the other areas of life.

A — Analytical Insights

Analytics and research

We rely on data, journals and decision patterns, not only on “I think so”. We move your processes from noise into system.

How I work

  • I do not sell “ready‑made schemes” or magic buttons — we build your system, not somebody else’s template.
  • I do not separate money and decisions from the body and nervous system — steps should be sustainable, not only “logically correct”.
  • I do not promise quick leaps — we work toward stable changes that hold without constant force of will.

Q&A before writing to me

❓ I’m not a trader or investor. Is this even for me?
Yes. If you have income, feel tired of carrying the money topic alone and keep postponing decisions “for later” — you are in my audience, even if you have never opened a trading chart.
⚠️ What if I have “chaos everywhere” and I feel ashamed to show it?
Chaos and shame are data about the system, not your “defect”. What matters to me is seeing the full picture so I can build a workable route, not judge you as a person.
🧩 What should I expect after the first session?
You leave not with “insight” alone, but with a map: where you are now, what hurts most, and which 1–3 steps make sense specifically in your context over the next weeks. Plus one week of chat support so you are not left alone with questions during the first steps.
📜 Is this about exactly where to invest?
Partly. I can explain asset classes, basic instruments and typical strategies, and help you look at an idea through the lens of risk, goals and your limits. But I do not build personal portfolios and I do not give formal recommendations on specific assets.

My style

  • INTJ: structure, logic and cause‑and‑effect matter more to me than “nice stories”.
  • I directly name the patterns that exhaust you and look for ways to change the system, not only to soothe you in the moment.
  • First I look at context and consequences of your actions, then we look for the smallest actions that can lead to different results.
  • For me, strategy is the search for the shortest and most effective path to change that your nervous system can actually tolerate in real life.
