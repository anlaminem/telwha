Who I Am

Not just about money, but about the mechanics of decisions

I am a financial psychologist and behavioral analyst. I combine work with the nervous system and cognitive biases with experience in trading, investments, financial instruments, and data analysis.

What matters to me is not only how a person allocates money, but also the context in which it happens: financial stress, war, instability, physical state, relationships, past losses, environment, and daily habits.

That is why I look not only at “what to do,” but also at whether your system can actually sustain that decision in real life.

  • I identify where patterns of thinking and emotion break strategy and keep repeating the same financial outcomes.
  • I translate your numbers, journals, and decision history into a clear map of risk, mistakes, and growth points.
  • I help build a system of actions and tools that your nervous system can sustain over time, not just “through willpower.”

Framework

What TELWHA Is

TELWHA is my working framework: six layers through which I look at decisions, money, behavior, and the real context of life.

T — Technological Integration

Technologies and tools

Financial tools, services, AI, journals, and dashboards help move decisions from your head into a real system that can be tracked and improved.

E — Emotional Intelligence

Emotions and the nervous system

We look at how stress, fatigue, traumatic experience, and expectations affect risk, money, and daily actions, and build protection against recurring breakdowns.

L — Layered Architecture

System layers

Capital, cash flow, habits, relationships, behavior, and work processes are broken down into layers so everything is not reduced to one chaotic “I have financial problems.”

W — Way & Strategy

Path and strategy

Strategy is not about stricter control, but about creating a manageable route of change that actually works under pressure, not only on paper.

H — Holistic Approach

Wholeness

Money and decisions do not exist separately from the body, relationships, work, fatigue, and environment. A sustainable system takes the whole context into account, not just the “correct logic.”

A — Analytical Insights

Analytics and research

We rely on data, journals, patterns, and actual behavior, not only on subjective impressions. This moves the process from noise into a system.

Approach

How I Work

  • I do not sell “ready-made schemes” or magic buttons — we build your system, not copy someone else’s template.
  • I do not separate money and decisions from the body and nervous system — the steps must be manageable, not only logically correct.
  • I do not promise quick breakthroughs — we work toward sustainable changes that hold without constant willpower.
Style

My Work Style

  • INTJ: structure, logic, and cause-and-effect relationships matter more than “beautiful stories.”
  • I name the exhausting patterns directly and look for a way to change the system, not just calm the moment.
  • First, I look at context and the consequences of actions, and then we look for the smallest steps that lead to different outcomes.
  • For me, strategy is the search for the shortest and most manageable path to change that the nervous system can actually sustain in real life.
FAQ

Q&A Before You Write to Me

❓I’m not a trader or an investor. Is this even for me?
Yes. If you have income, are tired of carrying the money topic on your own, and keep postponing decisions “for later” — you are part of my audience, even if you have never opened a chart in your life.
⚠️What if I have “chaos everywhere” and I’m ashamed to show it?
Chaos and shame are data about the system, not your defect. It matters to me to see the full picture so I can build a workable route from it, not judge you as a person.
🧩What should I expect after the first meeting?
You leave not just with ideas, but with a map: where you are now, what hurts most, and which 1–3 steps make sense in your context over the coming weeks.
📜Is this about exactly where to invest?
Partly. I can explain the logic of asset classes, instruments, and basic strategies, and also help you look at an idea through the lens of risk, goals, and your limits. But I do not build personal portfolios or provide formalized recommendations on specific assets.
